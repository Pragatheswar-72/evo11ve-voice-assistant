# Evo11ve AI Skills Program - Knowledge Base

Source: https://evo11ve.ai/for-ai (and the Evo11ve home page for contact details)

## About Evo11ve
Keywords: company, who is evo11ve, about us, platform, organisation
Evo11ve is an AI learning platform for school students. It offers a personal AI coach for students in Grades 6-12 (CBSE), JEE and NEET preparation, and 24/7 AI coaching. Evo11ve works with students, parents, teachers and schools. The AI Skills Program is Evo11ve's hands-on program that teaches school students how to use and build with AI.

## Program Overview
Keywords: what is the program, course, introduction, summary, details, about the course
The AI Skills Program is for students in Classes 6 to 12. The tagline is "Your Child Won't Just Use AI - They'll Master AI Responsibly."
Students learn to build, apply and question AI through hands-on projects. They start from a blank page and build a real project themselves.
No prior coding experience or AI knowledge is required.
The program teaches design thinking before using tools, and it also covers AI ethics and responsible use of AI.

## Who Can Join (Eligibility)
Keywords: eligibility, age, class, grade, standard, who can join, beginner, requirement, prerequisite
The program is meant for school students from Class 6 to Class 12.
No coding background or prior AI knowledge is needed to join.
Parents usually register on behalf of their child.

## Duration and Format
Keywords: duration, how long, hours, weeks, schedule, format, online, live classes, sessions, batch, cohort, seats
The program is 50 hours in total:
- 12 hours of live, interactive sessions led by mentors
- 36 hours of guided project building
- 2 hours of doubt-clearing sessions
Projects are completed over 6 weeks.
Seats are limited in each cohort (batch).

## Curriculum - What Students Learn
Keywords: syllabus, curriculum, modules, topics, what will my child learn, tools, ethics, design thinking
1. Practical AI tools: using AI for creativity, productivity, automation, research and problem-solving.
2. AI ethics: bias, privacy, responsible use, academic integrity and thoughtful use of new technology.
3. Design thinking: understanding the problem, asking good questions and making creative choices before opening any tool.
The page does not give a week-by-week syllabus and does not name specific software tools.
